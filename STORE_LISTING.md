# Rebble Store Listing — Minimal Plus

**Platform:** Pebble Time / Basalt, 144 × 168 color display.

**Description:** Minimal Plus is a customizable digital Pebble Time watchface.
Choose stacked or single-line time, adjust main clock size and spacing,
customize independent weekday and date sizes, weights, and colors, or mirror
the layout. Accent line width and vertical offset, battery position, icon,
percentage, sizes, and colors are all configurable. The watchface works
offline, and settings remain stored on the watch.

**v1.0.25 release notes:** Internal rendering optimizations improve the
performance of date layout fitting and smooth numeral painting, without
changing appearance or available settings.
