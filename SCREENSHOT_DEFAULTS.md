# Defaults

The screenshot-based defaults introduced in v1.0.23 remain in place, except the battery percentage was enabled at 30% compact size in v1.0.26. In v1.0.27, the default background changes to dark Oxford Blue (`#000055`) and both new horizontal centering toggles default to OFF; the accent and weekday/date initially retain their prior left alignment or right alignment under mirror mode.

Existing stored settings are preserved and override first-install defaults as usual.
